# Bulk Evidence-to-Claim Matrix — Working Version 0.1

**Status:** Exploratory; not a systematic review; not approved.  
**Purpose:** Keep claims traceable across the 40-step roadmap while the research is developed in bulk.  
**Rule:** “Evidence strength” is a provisional appraisal of fit/coverage at discovery stage, not a final GRADE-style certainty rating.

## Highest-priority verification queue

1. Trace the reported NSA 2022 literacy/numeracy percentages to original DPE/NSA tables and technical reports.
2. Inspect the full 2026 differentiated-classroom and Micro Skills working papers; extract design, outcomes, effect sizes, attrition, funding, and limitations.
3. Conduct BanglaJOL, university-repository, CAMPE/Education Watch, BANBEIS, DPE, NCTB, and ministry-archive searches with documented queries.
4. Verify exact National Education Policy 2010 and National Curriculum 2012 clauses and curriculum rollout chronology against primary records.
5. Update coaching, private tutoring, SSC/HSC washback, university admission, and credential-competition evidence with current Bangladesh data.
6. Review each major learning-science meta-analysis for population, outcomes, heterogeneity, risk of bias, and limits on transfer.
7. Develop a comparable evidence map for international systems without inferring causation from PISA rankings.
8. Add sources on development, motivation, curriculum subject domains, vocational pathways, climate/citizenship education, teacher professional development, and governance.
9. Build costs and equity analysis before any complete system proposal is drafted.
10. Run an adversarial review on every proposed system component and define what evidence would reverse the design choice.
