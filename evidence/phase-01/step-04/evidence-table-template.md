## Rules
- Separate a source's result from my interpretation.
- Trace load-bearing statistics to origin or mark **unverified at source**.
- Record instrument language and translation/version when relevant.
- State whether a measure directly captures the construct or uses a proxy.
- “Established evidence” must meet the threshold in `methods/step-04-evidence-synthesis-protocol.md`.
- For title-level and major design-driving claims, link the row to search logs, appraisal notes, counterevidence, devil's-advocate pass, and decision record.
