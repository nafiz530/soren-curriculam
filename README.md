# Soren Education System Research

**Author:** Md. Nafiz

## Official Research Title

> **Is our education system actually preparing students for the future, or is it mainly preparing students to pass examinations?**

This research investigates the question rather than assuming its answer. It will examine the strengths, limitations, purposes, evidence, and trade-offs of existing education systems before developing and testing any proposed framework.

## Research approach

The project begins with research rather than website development. Evidence, arguments, design decisions, sources, revisions, and limitations will be documented and version-controlled in this repository.

## Repository structure

Research documents are organized by phase and step, for example `research/phase-01/step-01/`. Step-specific source records use `sources/phase-01/step-01/` where relevant. The `Research Worklog/` directory contains the repository-wide `Main.md` change ledger and detailed worklogs for individual steps.

Research records are separated by function:
- `sources/` — source register and SRC-### provenance records;
- `evidence/` — evidence tables and search logs;
- `methods/` — approved cross-project protocols;
- `decisions/` — dated decisions, rationale, values, and revisit conditions;
- `research/phase-NN/step-NN/` — step manuscripts, plans, source maps, and review logs.

Step 3's unresolved questions are preserved as active obligations in the central roadmap.

## Mega research paper — preparation status

The next phase is preparation for a reader-facing full research paper under the working title *Exam-Oriented or Future-Ready? An Evidence Review of Learning Outcomes, Assessment Incentives and Reform Design in Bangladesh's School System.* The planned manuscript is a **structured narrative evidence review with realist-informed reasoning**, not a systematic review. It must use ordinary author–date citations and a complete reference list; repository source IDs (for example, SRC-037) are internal bookkeeping only and must never appear in the reader-facing manuscript. See [Manuscript Preparation Protocol](research/manuscript-preparation/README.md).

Do not label the future manuscript final or publication-ready until the gate is closed.
