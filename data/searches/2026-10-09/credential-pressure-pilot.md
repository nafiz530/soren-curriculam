## Exclusions and unresolved screening

- Search-result snippets and abstracts were used for discovery only, not as substitutes for full-text appraisal.
- A preliminary SSC “teaching to the test” result was not assigned a new source ID in this pass because the record and primary publication status require further verification.
- No claim is made that all relevant studies were found.
- Bangla-language searches and searches of BanglaJOL, CAMPE/Education Watch, BANBEIS, NCTB/Ministry primary records, and university repositories remain outstanding.
- Direct evidence on employers' use of SSC/HSC credentials, admission rules, and the relation between credentials and task-relevant competence remains outstanding.
