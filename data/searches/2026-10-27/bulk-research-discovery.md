## High-relevance sources surfaced

1. **Official National Education Policy 2010** — official-hosted PDF. Useful for stated aims; does not establish implementation or impact.  
   https://dpe.portal.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/dpe.portal.gov.bd/files/96a9aad5_16e6_414f_91e9_ba07a4746464/Education%20Policy%202010.pdf
2. **National Curriculum 2012** — NCTB PDF describing objective/learning-outcome design. Requires exact page and rollout verification.  
   https://nctb.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/nctb.portal.gov.bd/files/6d9b9671_f815_460c_b8ef_c58a1b829f55/English.pdf
3. **National Curriculum Framework 2021** — official PDF describing curriculum review and development process. Its account of process is not independent evaluation.  
   https://shed.portal.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/shed.portal.gov.bd/page/0b8eda3f_ce40_46c5_9e98_1ccda2ec348b/English.pdf
4. **National Student Assessment portal** — official source for primary assessment and learning trends. Original tables and technical reports still need to be inspected.  
   https://nsa.ipemis.dpe.gov.bd/?lang=en_EN
5. **UNICEF Innocenti (2024), Child Labour and Schooling** — secondary report includes NSA-related literacy/numeracy figures. Trace to primary NSA/DPE tables before using figures as load-bearing.  
   https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/innocenti/innocenti/media/9341/file/UNICEF-Innocenti-Child-labour-schooling-report-2024.pdf
6. **UNICEF Innocenti (2026), Drivers of Learning in Differentiated Classrooms** — recent Bangladesh intervention/working paper based on 24 secondary schools. Context-specific; not a national causal estimate.  
   https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/reports/drivers-learning-differentiated-classrooms
7. **UNICEF Innocenti (2026), Micro Skills in Bangladesh Secondary Schools** — first-year pilot in 120 secondary schools. Self-reported preparedness and attendance findings should not be confused with measured long-term competence.  
   https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/documents/micro-skills-bangladesh-secondary-schools
8. **Bangladesh examination-stress pilot (2020)** — online cross-sectional survey of 210 tertiary-level youths in Dhaka; limited generalizability.  
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.childyouth.2020.105254
9. **Bangladesh university-admission stress study** — cross-sectional online survey of 452 HSC graduates seeking admission; selection and causal limits.  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37181689/
10. **Disability-related school attendance study** — analysis of Bangladesh MICS 2019 data for 5,024 children with disabilities aged 6–17; association, not causation.  
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11501013/
11. **Siddik (2025), inclusive education and primary teachers** — qualitative evidence on training and infrastructure barriers.  
    https://doi.org/10.32204/jids.33.3_61
12. **Zaman & Raqib (2023), disability inclusion in secondary schools** — ten conveniently sampled schools in Dhaka and Rajshahi; useful context, not representative national evidence.  
    https://doi.org/10.3329/twjer.v48i1.67545
13. **Bangladesh Parliamentary Budget Helpdesk, education budget briefing** — official parliamentary material about FY2024–25 proposed allocations; allocation is not actual expenditure or evidence of effectiveness.  
    https://www.parliament.gov.bd/uploads/pages/budget-helpdesk-2024_015940_%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B7%E0%A6%BE.pdf
14. **Ministry of Education, Secondary and Higher Education Division budget tables** — official budget series; units, original/revised status, and fiscal-year comparability require checking.  
    https://shed.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/694146bea31054345f0fcaeb
15. **OECD PISA 2022 results** — international comparative evidence for participating systems, not a complete measure of educational value. Bangladesh should not be assigned a PISA 2022 score.  
    https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-i_53f23881-en.html
16. **UNESCO guidance on generative AI in education** — policy guidance, not causal evidence that a product improves learning.  
    https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research
17. **Campbell systematic review of class size** — international evidence with a limited meta-analytic subset; transfer to Bangladesh depends on context and implementation.  
    https://doi.org/10.4073/csr.2018.10
18. **2026 teacher homework-involvement meta-analysis** — 29 independent studies / 35,702 students; association should not be treated as a guaranteed intervention effect.  
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100837

## Screening and appraisal notes

- Prioritized official primary policy/statistical sources, peer-reviewed research, systematic reviews/meta-analyses, and recent Bangladesh-specific program evaluations.
- Did not accept a search snippet as sufficient for a definitive claim.
- Distinguished policy intent from implementation, correlation from causation, pilot samples from national estimates, self-report from measured outcomes, and allocated budgets from actual spending.
- Did not infer political motive from the chronology of curriculum changes.
- Did not assume that high-stakes examinations are inherently invalid or that alternatives are automatically more equitable.
- Did not treat international evidence as automatically transferable to Bangladeshi schools.
- Full independent source appraisal and source-register/SRC records remain outstanding.

## Important limitations

1. No systematic protocol with preregistered inclusion/exclusion criteria has been implemented.
2. No reliable total hit counts or full search exports were available through the search interface.
3. Bangla search coverage is a first pass, not exhaustive.
4. Source-level metadata, full texts, exact page/table references, funder/interest checks, and correction/retraction checks remain to be completed.
5. Primary data tables and original reports must be traced for load-bearing statistics.
6. Search engines and accessible repositories may under-index Bangla scholarship and gray literature.
7. This log supports discovery and triage only; it cannot justify an “exhaustive review” label.
