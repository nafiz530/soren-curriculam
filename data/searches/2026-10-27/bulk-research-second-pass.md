## Cautions
- Official policy establishes stated intent, not implementation impact.
- PISA is partial; Bangladesh has no PISA 2022 score.
- The 2022 Bangladesh tutoring study is qualitative, with 18 participants in two Sylhet higher-secondary institutions, not a national prevalence estimate.
- The 2024 formative-assessment meta-analysis reported Hedges' g = 0.25 across 258 effect sizes from 118 studies; it does not imply every continuous-assessment policy will work.
- National assessments measure selected constructs, not all of character, citizenship, creativity, wellbeing or future leadership.
