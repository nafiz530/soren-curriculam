- **Organization:** UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report team
- **Publication:** 2023 report
- **URL:** https://www.unesco.org/gem-report/en/technology
- **Language consulted:** English
- **Type:** Global monitoring report / policy evidence synthesis
- **Relevant topics:** Appropriate use of technology, evidence of effectiveness, access, governance, teacher support, and technology as a means rather than an end.
