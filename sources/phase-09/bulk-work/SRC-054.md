# SRC-054 — Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research

- **Authors:** Fengchun Miao and Wayne Holmes
- **Organization:** UNESCO
- **Publication:** 2023; page updated 16 January 2026
- **URL:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research
- **Language consulted:** English
- **Type:** International policy guidance
- **Relevant topics:** Human-centred governance, privacy, age-appropriate use, ethical validation, equity, and pedagogical design.
- **Appraisal:** Useful for identifying governance safeguards and policy questions. It is guidance, not a causal evaluation showing that generative AI improves learning.
- **Limits:** National law, infrastructure, language coverage, and school conditions vary; Bangladesh-specific legal and implementation analysis is needed.
- **Status:** Identified; recommendations and subsequent policy updates pending review.
