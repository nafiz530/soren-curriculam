# SRC-050 — The Science of Effective Learning With Spacing and Retrieval Practice

- **Authors:** Shana K. Carpenter, Steven C. Pan, Andrew C. Butler
- **Journal:** *Nature Reviews Psychology*, 1, 496–511
- **Publication:** 2 August 2022
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-022-00089-1
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-022-00089-1
- **Language:** English
- **Type:** Review article
- **Relevant claims:** Spacing, retrieval practice, metacognition, and applied educational implications.
- **Appraisal:** Useful review of two well-studied learning strategies and their mechanisms. The manuscript should distinguish retention effects from conceptual understanding, transfer, and classroom implementation.
- **Limits:** Not Bangladesh-specific; strategies must be adapted to subject, age, teacher workload, curriculum, and assessment. It does not support a universal method replacing instruction.
- **Status:** Identified; detailed claim extraction pending.
