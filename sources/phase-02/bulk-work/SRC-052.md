# SRC-052 — The Distributed Practice Effect on Classroom Learning

- **Authors:** Rhys D. Mawson and Sean H. K. Kang
- **Journal:** *Behavioral Sciences*, 15(6), 771
- **Publication:** 3 June 2025
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/bs15060771
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12189222/
- **Language:** English
- **Type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of applied classroom research
- **Search / sample:** Reported screening of over 3,000 articles; 22 reports and 31 effect sizes (N > 3,000) entered the quantitative synthesis.
- **Reported synthesis:** Distributed practice favoured massed practice, d = 0.54, 95% CI [0.31, 0.77].
- **Appraisal:** Directly relevant because inclusion required classroom studies with curriculum-relevant materials and timescales. The paper reports substantial heterogeneity (I² over 92% in the main analysis), limited moderator analysis, and potential small-study/selection issues.
- **Limits:** Average effect is not a guaranteed effect for every learner or topic; limited included studies and heterogeneity constrain precise moderator conclusions. Do not report the effect size without its uncertainty and limits.
- **Status:** Identified; full methods, bias, and heterogeneity appraisal pending.
