# SRC-010 — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Philosophy of Education

**Type:** Scholarly reference article  
**Publisher:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy  
**Canonical URL:** https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/education-philosophy/  
**Status:** Referenced; no local archival copy required at this stage

## Relevance

Provides an overview of philosophical debate about the nature and aims of education, including distinctions between education, training, growth, worthwhile knowledge, personal development, and competing educational purposes.

## Use in this research

Use this article as a scholarly map of the debate. When a particular argument becomes central to a claim or design decision, trace it to the underlying work where feasible.

## Caution

A reference article synthesizes a field; it is not itself an empirical study demonstrating that one definition or educational model produces superior outcomes.
