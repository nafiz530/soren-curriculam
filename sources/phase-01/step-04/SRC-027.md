# SRC-027 — Robert E. Slavin, Best-Evidence Synthesis

**Title:** “Perspectives on Evidence-Based Research in Education: What Works? Issues in Synthesizing Educational Program Evaluations”  
**Publication:** *Educational Researcher*, 2008, 37(1), 5–14  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X08314117  
**Type:** Peer-reviewed methodological article

## Contribution
Discusses synthesis of education-program evaluations using explicit standards and attention to study quality rather than undifferentiated vote-counting.

## Use
Supports a best-evidence synthesis when relevant studies vary in quality and direct pooling is inappropriate.

## Limits
The approach has its own scope and assumptions. It is not a universal substitute for question-specific synthesis design.
