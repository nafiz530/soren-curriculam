# SRC-032 — ROBIS: Risk of Bias in Systematic Reviews

**Resource:** ROBIS tool  
**Institutional page:** https://www.bristol.ac.uk/population-health-sciences/projects/robis/  
**Type:** Systematic-review risk-of-bias tool

## Contribution
Provides a structured approach to assessing risk of bias in systematic reviews.

## Use
Use when relying on an existing systematic review whose methods need independent appraisal.

## Limits
ROBIS assesses systematic reviews, not individual primary studies or every type of literature. It does not replace appraisal of the review's relevance and applicability.
