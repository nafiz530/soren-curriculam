# SRC-037 — Mahmud, Family Socioeconomic Determinants and Demand for English Tutoring

**Title:** “Family Socioeconomic Determinants and Students’ Demand for Private Supplementary Tutoring in English in Urban and Rural Bangladesh”  
**Author:** Rafsan Mahmud  
**Journal:** *Urban Education*, 53(7)  
**First published online:** 2020-12-14; journal issue published 2021  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1177/0013124520974332  
**Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical journal article  
**Status:** Referenced; publisher abstract reviewed

## Research design and reported finding

The article examines family socioeconomic factors associated with demand for private supplementary English tutoring in urban and rural Bangladesh. Its abstract reports a 2014–2015 survey in which 84.7% of urban and 60.7% of rural secondary students received private supplementary tutoring in English.

## Use in this project

The reported figures indicate historically substantial and geographically unequal participation in English tutoring in the surveyed population. Use the figures only with their exact date, subject, population, and study context. They may motivate analysis of affordability and unequal access, but cannot be presented as current national tutoring rates across all subjects.

## Appraisal and limitations

- The data are from 2014–2015; they are not current prevalence estimates.
- The reported rates are for English tutoring, not all supplementary tutoring.
- The abstract alone does not establish the survey sampling frame, response rate, weighting, or exact urban/rural coverage; inspect the full article before using the percentages as load-bearing statistics.
- Association between socioeconomic conditions and tutoring demand does not alone establish the effect of tutoring on learning outcomes.
