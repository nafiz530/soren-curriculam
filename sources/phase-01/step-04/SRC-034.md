# SRC-034 — Daniel Koretz, Measuring Up

**Title:** *Measuring Up: What Educational Testing Really Tells Us*  
**Author:** Daniel Koretz  
**Publisher/year:** Harvard University Press, 2008  
**Publisher catalog:** https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674035296  
**Type:** Scholarly book on educational assessment

## Contribution
Examines what educational test scores can and cannot establish, including limits of score interpretation and consequences of high-stakes use.

## Use
Inform appraisal of claims that exam scores represent broad learning, educational quality, or future readiness. It is relevant to construct validity and later assessment research.

## Limits
The book does not establish that all examinations are invalid or harmful. Claims must be matched to the test, use, population, and context.
