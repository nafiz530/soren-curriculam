# SRC-025 — Ray Pawson, Evidence-Based Policy: The Promise of Realist Synthesis

**Publication:** *Evaluation*, 2002, 8(3), 340–358  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1177/135638902401462448  
**Type:** Methodological research article

## Contribution
Explains realist synthesis as an approach to building policy explanations from prior research, emphasizing that evidence does not speak for itself and that mechanisms and context matter.

## Use
Supports asking what works, for whom, in what circumstances, through which mechanisms, and with what limitations.

## Limits
It does not validate any particular education intervention. A realist approach still depends on careful selection, appraisal, and testing of underlying evidence.
