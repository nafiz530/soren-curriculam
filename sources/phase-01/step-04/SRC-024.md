# SRC-024 — Nancy Cartwright and Jeremy Hardie, Evidence-Based Policy

**Title:** *Evidence-Based Policy: A Practical Guide to Doing It Better*  
**Authors:** Nancy Cartwright and Jeremy Hardie  
**Publisher/year:** Oxford University Press, 2012  
**URL:** https://academic.oup.com/book/11611  
**Type:** Scholarly monograph

## Contribution
Argues that evidence of an intervention's effectiveness does not automatically establish that it will work elsewhere. Policy judgement must examine causal conditions, capacities, and context.

## Use
Ground the transferability rule: evidence that an intervention worked elsewhere is a reason to investigate, not proof that it will work in Bangladesh.

## Limits
This is a methodological/philosophical work, not a Bangladesh-specific intervention study. It does not establish which reforms work locally.
