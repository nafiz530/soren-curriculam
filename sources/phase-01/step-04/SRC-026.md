# SRC-026 — Ray Pawson and Nick Tilley, Realistic Evaluation

**Title:** *Realistic Evaluation*  
**Authors:** Ray Pawson and Nick Tilley  
**Publisher/year:** SAGE, 1997  
**URL:** https://uk.sagepub.com/en-gb/eur/realistic-evaluation/book205276  
**Type:** Scholarly methods book

## Contribution
Develops a theory-driven approach to evaluation that investigates how mechanisms interact with context to produce outcomes.

## Use
Inform mechanism-focused questions when comparing educational interventions and local conditions.

## Limits
The framework is not a shortcut around empirical scrutiny and does not guarantee that a proposed mechanism is correct.
