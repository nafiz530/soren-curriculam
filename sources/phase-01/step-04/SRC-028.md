# SRC-028 — Popay et al., Guidance on the Conduct of Narrative Synthesis

**Title:** *Guidance on the Conduct of Narrative Synthesis in Systematic Reviews*  
**Authors:** Janet Popay et al.  
**Publisher/year:** ESRC Methods Programme, 2006  
**URL:** https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/media/lancaster-university/content-assets/documents/fhm/dhr/chir/NSsynthesisguidanceVersion1-April2006.pdf  
**Type:** Methods guidance

## Contribution
Provides a structured approach to narrative synthesis, including developing a theory, preliminary synthesis, exploring relationships in the evidence, and assessing robustness.

## Use
Structure a transparent synthesis when measures, contexts, and study designs are too heterogeneous for one pooled estimate.

## Limits
Narrative synthesis can still be selective or overly interpretive if search, appraisal, and reasoning are not explicit. It does not prove any substantive education claim.
