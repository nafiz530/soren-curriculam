# SRC-030 — Critical Appraisal Skills Programme (CASP)

**Resource:** CASP critical appraisal checklists  
**Publisher:** Critical Appraisal Skills Programme  
**URL:** https://casp-uk.net/casp-tools-checklists/  
**Type:** Practical appraisal tools

## Contribution
Provides structured prompts for appraisal of several research designs, including qualitative studies, randomized trials, cohort studies, and systematic reviews.

## Use
Select the checklist that fits the study design and use it to guide questions, not as a substitute for judgement or as a universal score.

## Limits
Checklist coverage varies by design and version. Confirm the current checklist and its fit before applying it.
