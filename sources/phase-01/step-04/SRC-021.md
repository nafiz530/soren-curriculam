# SRC-021 — What Works Clearinghouse Procedures and Standards Handbook, Version 5.0

**Publisher:** U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse  
**Version:** 5.0, August 2022; revised December 2022  
**Source type:** Official methodological handbook for reviewing education intervention research  
**Official handbook page:** https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Handbooks  
**Direct PDF:** https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/Docs/referenceresources/Final_WWC-HandbookVer5_0-0-508.pdf

## Main contribution

The What Works Clearinghouse reviews and summarizes research quality for educational programmes, products, practices, and policies. Its handbook sets out procedures and standards used to review studies and characterize findings.

## Intended use in this project

Use the handbook as an education-specific example of why research appraisal needs explicit procedures rather than impressions. Consult it when later chapters examine intervention effectiveness, study design, and the limits of causal evidence.

## Limits and cautions

- WWC's standards apply within its defined remit; they are not a universal hierarchy for philosophy, history, policy text, qualitative experience, or every form of educational research.
- Its methods and criteria arise from a particular institutional context. They must not be applied mechanically to Bangladesh without examining context and the research question.
- A handbook describing standards does not itself establish the effectiveness of any particular intervention.

## Claims this source can support

- WWC has published a versioned set of procedures and standards for reviewing education intervention studies.
- Explicit standards can make research appraisal more transparent and consistent.

## Claims it cannot support by itself

- That a specific educational practice is effective.
- That all high-quality education research must use one study design.
- That results from studies reviewed under WWC standards automatically transfer to Bangladesh.

## Appraisal note

This is a primary institutional source for WWC's own procedures. It is authoritative for describing that framework, not a neutral verdict on every methodological debate.
