# SRC-029 — Gough, Oliver, and Thomas, An Introduction to Systematic Reviews

**Title:** *An Introduction to Systematic Reviews*, 2nd edition  
**Authors:** David Gough, Sandy Oliver, and James Thomas  
**Publisher/year:** SAGE, 2017  
**URL:** https://uk.sagepub.com/en-gb/eur/an-introduction-to-systematic-reviews/book245742  
**Type:** Research-methods textbook

## Contribution
Introduces systematic-review reasoning, review questions, searching, appraisal, synthesis, and matching review methods to questions.

## Use
Clarify what is required before describing a review as systematic and inform honest small-scale review design.

## Limits
A textbook cannot establish the quality of a specific review without examining its actual procedures.
