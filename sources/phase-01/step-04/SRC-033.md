# SRC-033 — AMSTAR 2

**Title:** “AMSTAR 2: a critical appraisal tool for systematic reviews that include randomised or non-randomised studies of healthcare interventions, or both”  
**Authors:** Beverly J. Shea et al.  
**Publication:** *BMJ*, 2017, 358, j4008  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.j4008  
**Type:** Systematic-review appraisal tool

## Contribution
Offers structured critical appraisal for systematic reviews of randomized and non-randomized healthcare intervention studies.

## Use
Treat as an example of domain-specific review appraisal and apply it only when its intended scope fits. It is not the default tool for education reviews.

## Limits
AMSTAR 2 was designed for healthcare intervention reviews. Do not mechanically apply it to education literature outside its scope; ROBIS or another appropriate tool may fit better.
