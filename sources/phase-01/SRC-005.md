# SRC-005 — UNICEF Education Strategy 2026: Shaping the Future of Learning

**Type:** Education strategy  
**Year:** 2026  
**Status:** Referenced; archival PDF requested

## Why this source matters to Step 1

This is the most recent source currently in the Step 1 register and directly addresses the question of whether education systems designed for earlier eras can prepare children for future conditions. It frames UNICEF's current strategy around learning, equity and skills development.

## Methodological caution

The document is a strategy, not a neutral systematic review. It should be treated as an institutional position informed by UNICEF experience, while individual empirical claims should be traced to their cited evidence.
