# SRC-006 — World Bank Bangladesh Learning Poverty Brief 2024

**Type:** Country evidence brief  
**Year:** 2024  
**Status:** Collected; local PDF and dataset archived

## Why this source matters to Step 1

This source provides Bangladesh-specific evidence about foundational reading proficiency, learning deprivation, and schooling deprivation. It helps me avoid defining educational problems entirely through international frameworks or abstract philosophy.

## Important limitation

Learning Poverty is a specific indicator focused on foundational reading and access. It is not a complete measure of educational quality, student development, or system success.

## Initial evidence note

The 2024 brief reports a Learning Poverty estimate of 51% for Bangladesh using the latest available data at that time, with methodological and data-availability qualifications. I must preserve the indicator's definition, data vintage, and limitations whenever I use this estimate.
