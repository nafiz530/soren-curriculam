# SRC-006 — World Bank Bangladesh Learning Poverty Brief 2024

**Type:** Country evidence brief  
**Year:** 2024  
**Status:** Referenced; archival PDF and dataset requested

## Why this source matters to Step 1

It provides Bangladesh-specific evidence about foundational reading proficiency, learning deprivation and schooling deprivation. It helps prevent the project from defining educational problems entirely through international frameworks or abstract philosophy.

## Important limitation

Learning Poverty is a specific indicator focused on foundational reading and access. It should not be treated as a complete measure of educational quality, student development or system success.

## Initial evidence note

The 2024 brief reports a Learning Poverty estimate of 51% for Bangladesh using the latest available data at that time, with important methodological and data-availability qualifications. The exact estimate, data vintage and limitations must be preserved when cited.
