# SRC-017 — Review Committee Report on Secondary and Higher Secondary Education

**Title:** *Report of the Review Committee Established to Formulate a Vision for the Transformation of the Secondary and Higher Secondary Education System (with an Integrated Whole-of-Schooling Perspective), Volume I: Vision for Learning Transformation — Setting Direction, Priorities, and Governing Commitments*  
**Publisher:** Secondary and Higher Education Division, Ministry of Education, Government of Bangladesh  
**Publication:** February 2026  
**Type:** Official review committee report  
**Status:** Referenced; official PDF identified

## Step 3 relevance

This report documents a formal review process focused on transforming the secondary and higher-secondary education system. It is evidence that a specialized committee undertook substantial education reform work during the interim period, even though this is distinct from a single sector-wide education reform commission.

## Use

Read the report's mandate, membership, methods, consultation, proposed principles, and recommendations. Distinguish its proposed vision from government adoption and implementation. Compare its approach with the National Education Policy 2010 and subsequent policy/curriculum decisions.

## Caution

Publication of a committee report does not establish that all recommendations were adopted, implemented, funded, or evaluated.
