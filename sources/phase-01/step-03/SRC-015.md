# SRC-015 — NCTB Official Curriculum and Assessment Records, 2025–2026

**Publisher:** National Curriculum and Textbook Board (NCTB), Bangladesh  
**Type:** Official curriculum documents and webpages  
**Status:** Referenced; official pages and a primary curriculum PDF identified

## Step 3 relevance

These records help establish what curriculum and assessment documents NCTB officially published after the 2023 rollout. They are important for separating a curriculum framework from its grade-by-grade implementation, later revisions, textbook publication, and national education policy.

## Official sources identified

- [NCTB official website and curriculum archive](https://nctb.gov.bd/)
- [National Curriculum 2021 — Primary Level (Revised 2025), PDF](https://nctb.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/nctb.portal.gov.bd/notices/9e5b0465_09d6_4640_ab74_ca0152f120fe/2025-06-03-12-11-64d5eaf21db10cb06886841a3d4594f0.pdf)
- [Revised syllabus and assessment guidance for 2026](https://nctb.portal.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dce3933eb65569e12972)
- [NCTB's 2025 secondary and higher-secondary textbook lists](https://nctb.portal.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dd8c933eb65569e156cb)

## Use

Record the exact title, issuing body, date, grade range, and status of each primary document. Build a verified chronology for the 2012 curriculum changes, the 2021 framework, the rollout from 2023, and later adjustments. Distinguish published design from classroom implementation and learning outcomes.

## Caution

The existence of revised curriculum documents does not by itself prove how fully they were implemented, why a revision was made, or whether it improved learning. The 2012 revision and exact grade-by-grade rollout still require direct archival verification.
