# SRC-019 — Publicly Stated Curriculum Reform Plans, 2026

**Publisher:** Bangladesh Sangbad Sangstha (BSS), reporting government statements  
**Date:** 30 August 2026; published 4 September 2026  
**Type:** Contemporary news report on current government plans  
**Status:** Referenced

## Step 3 relevance

BSS reported that the government planned to redesign the curriculum for the 2028 academic session and introduce new subjects, including topics related to the Fourth Industrial Revolution, artificial intelligence, and sports from the 2027 academic year. This provides a dated example of planned curriculum change after the political transition from the interim government to an elected government.

## Use

Track the proposal's public rationale, formal status, consultation process, evidence base, and eventual implementation. Evaluate it by the same standards as earlier reforms: educational justification, historical accuracy, rights, feasibility, transparent consultation, and evidence of outcomes.

## Caution

A reported plan is not a formally adopted curriculum, and a proposal's timing does not prove partisan motivation.
