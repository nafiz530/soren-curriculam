# SRC-010 — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Philosophy of Education

**Type:** Scholarly reference article  
**Publisher:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy  
**Canonical URL:** https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/education-philosophy/  
**Status:** Referenced; no local archival copy required at this stage

## Step 3 relevance

Maps philosophical debates about the nature and aims of education, including personal autonomy, flourishing, economic productivity, democratic competence, equality, relationships, and the justification and prioritization of competing aims.

## Use

Use the article as a scholarly map, then trace important arguments to original publications when they materially affect the research's conclusions or design decisions.

## Caution

The article synthesizes an active field of debate. It is not an empirical study proving that one educational aim or system is superior.
