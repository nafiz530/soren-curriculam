## Step 3 relevance

Describes UNICEF's current strategic priorities around learning, equity, and skills development for children and adolescents amid technological, social, and environmental change.

## Use

Use it to identify UNICEF's stated institutional priorities and the problems its strategy seeks to address.

## Caution

A strategy document records priorities and intended direction. It does not by itself establish that every priority is universally optimal or that a particular intervention has achieved its intended impact.
