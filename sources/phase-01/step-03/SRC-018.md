# SRC-018 — Primary and Non-Formal Education Reform Advisory Committee

**Committee/report date:** 10 February 2025  
**Type:** Advisory committee report, documented through Bangladesh Sangbad Sangstha (BSS) coverage  
**Status:** Referenced; report submission and recommendations reported

## Step 3 relevance

The committee submitted recommendations to the Chief Adviser on primary and non-formal education. BSS reported that the recommendations covered eight areas, emphasized inclusive education and targeted budget support, and included immediate, medium-term, and long-term actions.

## Use

Treat the committee as a specialized reform body. Record its mandate, recommendations, and subsequent government response separately from any broad education-sector commission or national education policy.

## Caution

News coverage of report submission does not establish implementation or impact. The original committee report and official response should be located before making detailed claims about individual recommendations.
