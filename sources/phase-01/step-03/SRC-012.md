# SRC-012 — Ronald Dore, The Diploma Disease

**Title:** *The Diploma Disease: Education, Qualification and Development*  
**Author:** Ronald Philip Dore  
**Original publication:** 1976  
**Type:** Scholarly monograph  
**Status:** Referenced; bibliographic records identified, full text not archived

## Step 3 relevance

Dore examines how schooling can become increasingly organized around obtaining qualifications, especially when desirable jobs are scarce and educational credentials influence access to employment. The book includes comparative cases from Britain, Japan, Sri Lanka, and Kenya, making its South Asian context relevant to questions about examination pressure and qualification-seeking.

## Use

Use Dore to formulate and test mechanisms that may help explain credential competition, examination preparation, and the influence of selection systems on teaching. Compare the argument with contemporary Bangladesh evidence rather than assuming that the 1976 analysis transfers unchanged.

## Caution

This book is a major comparative argument, not direct evidence of present-day Bangladesh's entire education system. Do not claim that every examination is harmful or that credential competition has a single cause.
