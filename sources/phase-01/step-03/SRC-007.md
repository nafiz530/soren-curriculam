# SRC-007 — Bangladesh National Education Policy 2010

**Title:** *National Education Policy 2010*  
**Publisher:** Government of Bangladesh, Ministry of Education  
**Year:** 2010  
**Repository artifact:** sources/documents/SRC-007-bangladesh-national-education-policy-2010.pdf  
**Status:** Collected

## Step 3 relevance

Primary national policy source for examining Bangladesh's formally stated educational aims, principles, and commitments.

## Use

Read the primary document directly before making specific claims about its wording. Treat it as evidence of stated policy intent. Compare those aims with implementation and outcome evidence in later research steps.

## Caution

The publication of a policy does not demonstrate that its goals were implemented, adequately funded, or achieved. Do not use policy language as a substitute for evidence about learner experience or educational outcomes.
