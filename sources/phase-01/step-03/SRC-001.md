# SRC-001 — UNESCO: Reimagining our futures together

**Title:** *Reimagining our futures together: A new social contract for education*  
**Publisher:** UNESCO, International Commission on the Futures of Education  
**Year:** 2021  
**Canonical page:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/reimagining-our-futures-together-new-social-contract-education  
**Original PDF:** sources/documents/SRC-001-unesco-reimagining-our-futures-together-2021.pdf  
**Status:** Collected

## Step 3 relevance

Offers a normative argument for rethinking education's collective purposes, including education throughout life and education as a public and common good.

## Use

Use it to describe UNESCO's proposed framework and its reasoning about shared futures. Distinguish the report's normative position from empirical claims about the effectiveness of specific policies.

## Caution

This is an influential commission report, not a universally binding definition of education's purpose or an experimental evaluation of a particular education system.
