# SRC-016 — Public Reporting on the Proposed Education Policy and Commission, 2024–2025

**Type:** Contemporary news reports  
**Status:** Referenced; reports identified, primary government confirmation still required

## Step 3 relevance

These reports document public statements about a possible replacement for the National Education Policy 2010 and a proposed education reform commission. They matter to the policy timeline, but the reports do not agree on whether a commission would be formed.

## Reports identified

- [The Daily Star, 18 December 2024 — “Awami League’s education policy to be scrapped”](https://online.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/politics/news/als-edn-policy-be-scrapped-3779841): reports a stated intention to scrap the 2010 policy and announce a commission.
- [Views Bangladesh, 22 January 2025 — “Government has no plans to form education reform commission: Adviser”](https://viewsbangladesh.com/en/government-has-no-plans-to-form-education-reform-commission-adviser/): reports the education adviser's statement that there were no plans for such a broad commission at that time and mentions a possible advisory council instead.
- [Prothom Alo, 11 August 2025 — report on the absence of a sector-wide commission](https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/bangladesh/f59m3g1rpi): reports that a broad education-sector reform commission had not been constituted during the interim government's first year.
- [BSS, 10 February 2025 — Primary and Non-Formal Education Reform Advisory Committee submits report](https://www.bssnews.net/news/245447): documents a separate advisory committee's recommendations.
- [Ministry of Education, February 2026 — Secondary and Higher Secondary Education System Review Committee report](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-shed/2026/1/8832c044-7ad4-44db-b88b-31ad1467083a.pdf): an official review report for a distinct committee.

## Use

Treat these as evidence of public reporting and the need for verification, not as a substitute for official records. Seek a gazette, ministry order, membership notice, terms of reference, official report, or other primary record before stating that a commission was formally constituted or completed its work.

## Caution

The evidence supports a precise distinction: a broad education-sector commission was reported as proposed but later reporting says it was not constituted, while separate primary and secondary-level reform committees did produce recommendations or reports. Do not infer political motives from the timing of announcements alone.
