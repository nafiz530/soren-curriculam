- **Organization:** World Bank
- **Title:** *Bangladesh — Learning Poverty Brief 2024*

- **Official record:** https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099090524113159222
- **Type:** Standardized indicator brief
- **Definition:** Learning poverty summarizes children unable to read and understand a simple text by around age ten, combining learning deprivation and schooling deprivation under the indicator method.
