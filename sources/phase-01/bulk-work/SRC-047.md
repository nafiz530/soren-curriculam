# SRC-047 — Bangladesh Education Budget Briefing, FY2024–25

- **Organization:** Bangladesh Parliament, Budget Helpdesk
- **Title:** Education budget briefing (Bangla-language PDF)
- **URL:** https://www.parliament.gov.bd/uploads/pages/budget-helpdesk-2024_015940_%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%B7%E0%A6%A7%E0%A6%A8.pdf
- **Fiscal-year scope:** FY2023–24 original/revised allocation and FY2024–25 proposed allocation
- **Language consulted:** Bangla
- **Source type:** Parliamentary budget briefing / secondary presentation of budget data
- **Relevant claims:** Education-sector proposed allocations and allocations by ministry/division.
- **Appraisal:** Useful official-source entry point for fiscal context. Budget proposals are not actual expenditure, per-student resources, or evidence of effectiveness. Cross-year comparisons require consistent units and original/revised/actual distinctions.
- **Limits:** A budget briefing is not a complete expenditure-incidence analysis. Verify each figure against Ministry of Finance budget documents, revised estimates, actual expenditure, GDP/budget denominator, inflation, enrolment, and distribution across levels and regions.
