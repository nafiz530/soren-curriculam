# SRC-044 — Micro Skills in Bangladesh Secondary Schools

- **Authors:** Margi Bhatt, Hossne Ara, Ursula Hinostroza, Muhammad Monirul Islam, Md. Mostafa Kamal, Sheikh Nishat
- **Organization:** UNICEF Innocenti / programme partners
- **Title:** *Micro Skills in Bangladesh Secondary Schools: Year 1 Findings — Implementation lessons and early outcomes of school-based life skills pilot programme*
- **Publication:** 2026; working paper
- **Stable URL:** https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/documents/micro-skills-bangladesh-secondary-schools
- **Language consulted:** English
- **Source type:** Bangladesh-specific programme working paper
- **Relevant claims:** Career planning, digital learning and safety, social-emotional learning, green skills, implementation and participation.
- **Population / setting:** First-year programme across 120 secondary schools, estimated reach of 16,000 students.
- **Reported findings:** 82% of students reportedly felt better prepared for the future; 57% attended four sessions or fewer; implementation varied by school.
- **Appraisal:** Useful contextual evidence for feasibility and student-reported outcomes. The 82% figure is self-reported preparedness, not an independent measure of skill acquisition, retention, or long-term life outcomes.
- **Potential bias / interests:** Programme implementer / development-partner reporting context; inspect full paper for evaluation design, funding, instruments, and missing data.
- **Limits:** First-year findings; attendance and exposure varied; self-report cannot establish causal impact. No national generalization without sampling and comparison details.
- **Review status:** Identified; full-text critical appraisal pending.
