# SRC-043 — Drivers of Learning in Differentiated Classrooms

- **Authors:** Dita Nugroho, Hossne Ara, Muhammad Monirul Islam, Sheikh Nishat Nazmi, Afia Sultana
- **Organization:** UNICEF Innocenti / Government of Bangladesh programme context
- **Title:** *Drivers of Learning in Differentiated Classrooms: Lessons from two years of piloting a differentiated teaching intervention in Bangladesh secondary schools*
- **Publication:** September 2026; working paper
- **Stable URL:** https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/reports/drivers-learning-differentiated-classrooms
- **Language consulted:** English
- **Source type:** Bangladesh-specific programme working paper / implementation evidence
- **Relevant claims:** Diagnostic checks matched to learner level; varied learning materials; group work and individual accountability; class-size and teacher-support conditions.
- **Population / setting:** Two-year pilot in 24 Bangladeshi secondary schools.
- **Methods:** Public summary describes programme evidence and classroom practices; full PDF and technical methods must be inspected before causal claims are accepted.
- **Reported findings:** Checks matched to students' current level were most consistently linked to better learning; varied materials helped especially in mathematics; group work helped in Bangla but not mathematics when individual accountability was weak; very large classes reduced effectiveness; teacher confidence in checking progress declined over the year.
- **Appraisal:** High contextual relevance; promising evidence on implementation mechanisms. Treat as a programme working paper, not a national representative estimate. Verify design, outcomes, comparison condition, missing data, uncertainty, and independence before causal interpretation.
- **Potential bias / interests:** UNICEF and government programme context; inspect report disclosures and evaluation arrangements.
- **Limits:** Only 24 schools; results depend on intervention, training, school selection, outcomes, and implementation. Do not generalize directly to every subject, grade, or school.
- **Review status:** Identified; full-text critical appraisal pending.
