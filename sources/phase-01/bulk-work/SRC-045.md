# SRC-045 — Examination-Related Stress Among Bangladeshi Youths

- **Authors:** Anowara Rayhan Arusha; Raaj Kishore Biswas
- **Title:** “Prevalence of stress, anxiety and depression due to examination in Bangladeshi youths: A pilot study”
- **Journal:** *Children and Youth Services Review*, volume 116, article 105254
- **Publication date:** September 2020
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.childyouth.2020.105254
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7367775/
- **Language consulted:** English
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed pilot, cross-sectional survey
- **Population / setting:** Online survey of 210 tertiary-level youths aged 17–25 in Dhaka, conducted in May 2020.
- **Measures / methods:** Modified DASS-21 and cross-sectional statistical analysis.
- **Relevant claims:** Potential associations between examination-related stress and student circumstances, sleep, diet, and family interaction.
- **Appraisal:** Directly relevant to student well-being but limited by pilot sample, online recruitment, geographic concentration, age range, cross-sectional design, and self-report. Associations cannot establish causation.
- **Limits:** Cannot estimate national prevalence for all students; cannot be generalized to secondary-school students without separate evidence; cannot isolate examinations as the sole cause.
- **Review status:** Identified; full-text instrument and sampling appraisal pending.
