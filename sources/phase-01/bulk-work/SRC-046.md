# SRC-046 — Disability and School Attendance in Bangladesh

- **Title:** “Factors affecting school enrolment and attendance for children with disabilities in Bangladesh: Evidence from a cross-sectional survey”
- **Publication:** 2024
- **Stable URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11501013/
- **Language consulted:** English
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed quantitative analysis
- **Data / population:** Bangladesh Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey 2019; reported analysis includes 5,024 children with disabilities aged 6–17.
- **Relevant claims:** Associations between child/household/geographic characteristics and school attendance among children with disabilities.
- **Appraisal:** Use of a large national survey is a strength compared with convenience samples. However, cross-sectional associations are not causal effects; definitions, weighting, missingness, model specification, and disability measures need review in the full paper.
- **Limits:** Does not by itself establish the causal impact of any single policy or intervention; MICS data are from 2019 and may not reflect current conditions.
- **Review status:** Identified; full-text methods and table-level appraisal pending.
