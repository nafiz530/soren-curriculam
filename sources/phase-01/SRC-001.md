# SRC-001 — UNESCO: Reimagining our futures together

**Type:** International commission report  
**Year:** 2021  
**Canonical source:** UNESCO Futures of Education  
**Status:** Collected; local PDF archived

## Why this source matters to Step 1

This is a major normative and foresight source for examining educational purpose, the relationship between education and society, possible futures, and the principle that educational visions require contextualization.

UNESCO presents the report as a starting point rather than a blueprint. This distinction matters to my method: I can use the report to frame questions and identify competing purposes of education, but I should not automatically turn its recommendations into design requirements.

## Relevant Step 1 questions

- What is education for?
- What purposes can education serve?
- How should education relate to society and future uncertainty?
- Which claims are normative rather than empirical?

## Initial evidence note

The report frames education around the right to education and education as a public/common good, and discusses multiple possible futures rather than treating one forecast as inevitable. These are positions and frameworks advanced by the commission, not empirical proof that one education model is universally optimal.
