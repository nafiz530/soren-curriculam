# SRC-001 — UNESCO: Reimagining our futures together

**Type:** International commission report  
**Year:** 2021  
**Canonical source:** UNESCO Futures of Education  
**Status:** Referenced; archival PDF requested

## Why this source matters to Step 1

This is a major normative and foresight source for examining educational purpose, the relationship between education and society, possible futures, and the principle that educational visions require contextualization.

UNESCO explicitly describes the report as a starting point rather than a blueprint. That distinction is important for our methodology: the report can inform the research question and competing purposes of education, but its recommendations must not automatically become design requirements.

## Relevant Step 1 questions

- What is education for?
- What purposes can education serve?
- How should education relate to society and future uncertainty?
- Which claims are normative rather than empirical?

## Initial evidence note

The report frames education around the right to education and education as a public/common good, and discusses multiple possible futures rather than treating one forecast as inevitable. These are positions/frameworks of the commission, not empirical proof that one education model is universally optimal.
