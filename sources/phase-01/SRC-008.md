# SRC-008 — World Bank Global Education Policy Dashboard

**Type:** Policy/system measurement framework  
**Year:** 2026  
**Status:** Referenced; archival report/export requested if available

## Why this source matters to Step 1

It can contribute a system-level perspective on policy conditions and bottlenecks, helping us avoid defining education solely through curriculum or classroom practice.

## Methodological caution

Dashboard indicators are measurement constructs. They require attention to definitions, data coverage, comparability and whether an observed association can support causal claims.
