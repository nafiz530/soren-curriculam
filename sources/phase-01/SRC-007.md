# SRC-007 — Bangladesh National Education Policy 2010

**Type:** National education policy  
**Year:** 2010  
**Status:** Referenced; archival PDF requested

## Why this source matters to Step 1

The policy is essential for understanding Bangladesh's own stated purposes, principles and intended direction for education.

## Methodological caution

A national policy establishes formal aims and commitments. It does not, by itself, establish that those aims were implemented or that implementation produced the intended outcomes.
