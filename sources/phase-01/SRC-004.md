# SRC-004 — UNICEF Every child learns

**Type:** Education strategy  
**Year:** 2019  
**Status:** Referenced; archival PDF requested

## Why this source matters to Step 1

UNICEF's strategy explicitly frames the learning crisis in terms of meaningful learning outcomes and preparation for life, work and active citizenship.

## Methodological caution

This is an institutional strategy and therefore combines diagnosis, normative commitments and recommended action. Its claims must be separated into empirical evidence, interpretation and strategy.
