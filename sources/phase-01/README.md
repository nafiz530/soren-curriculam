# Phase 1 Sources — Research Foundation

This folder contains sources introduced specifically for Phase 1.

## Step 1 source questions

Sources should help us answer:

1. How do authoritative institutions define or frame education?
2. What purposes are attributed to education?
3. What outcomes are considered legitimate?
4. What tensions exist between individual, social, economic, civic and human purposes?
5. What evidence exists about Bangladesh's stated education goals and actual learning conditions?
6. Which claims are normative, empirical, or predictive?
7. What should be included in the scope of the Soren research, and what should remain outside it?

No source is treated as automatically correct. Institutional authority establishes provenance, not infallibility.
