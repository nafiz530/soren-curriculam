# SRC-011 — UNESCO Institute for Statistics: International Standard Classification of Education (ISCED 2011)

**Type:** International statistical classification/framework  
**Year:** 2012  
**Canonical URL:** https://uis.unesco.org/sites/default/files/documents/international-standard-classification-of-education-isced-2011-en.pdf  
**Status:** Referenced; official PDF identified

## Relevance

Provides operational definitions for education programmes and learning, and a common framework for classifying education programmes and qualifications for international comparison.

## Use in this research

Use ISCED to clarify the operational meaning of education programmes and the distinction between programme participation, learning objectives, and qualification. It is particularly useful when the research discusses formal education and cross-country comparisons.

## Caution

ISCED is designed for classification and statistical comparability. It does not settle every philosophical question about the purpose, value, or quality of education.
