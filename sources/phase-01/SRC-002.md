# SRC-002 — OECD Learning Compass 2030

**Type:** Framework / concept-note series  
**Year:** 2019 onward  
**Status:** Referenced; archival PDF requested

## Why this source matters to Step 1

The Learning Compass provides a major international framework for discussing student agency, knowledge, skills, attitudes, values and well-being in future-oriented education.

## Methodological caution

A framework describing desirable educational outcomes is not itself evidence that implementing every component causes better outcomes. We will use it primarily as a conceptual source in Step 1 and later compare its propositions against empirical research.
