# Source Register

**Purpose:** Master index of research sources. This is a living bibliography, not a list of conclusions.

## Access and appraisal legend

**Access** records whether the source artifact is locally archived or merely identified:
- **collected** — a source artifact is archived in the repository.
- **referenced** — a canonical source is identified and used, but no local copy is recorded.
- **pending** — access or source identity remains unresolved.

**Appraisal** records the current review state separately. “Not started,” “pending,” and “identity to verify” are not quality judgments. A collected source is not automatically appraised or accepted.

## Initial source-selection observation

The source base mixes international normative work, future-oriented frameworks, child-focused system strategy, country-level evidence, Bangladesh policy, system measurement, scholarly philosophy, and international statistical classification. This variety helps frame the questions, but it does not by itself guarantee a balanced evidence base. Critical scholarship and counterevidence must still be actively sought.

## Additional data archive

The World Bank Learning Poverty Global Database workbook is a separate artifact assigned **SRC-074** under the one-ID-per-artifact rule. Its vintage is the **2021 update covering data from 2001–2019**, not a 2024 release. The current repository filename includes `2024` and is therefore misleading; rename it only after confirming the exact artifact path. The Bangladesh values reported from this workbook are 57.2% (2015) and 58.1% (2017). Do not compare them as a trend with SRC-006's 51% COVID-closure-period brief estimate: the assessment and threshold differ. A South Asia rows CSV was also exported; its exact repository path still needs to be recorded.

## Archival rules

- Do not rename a source downloaded from an official provider into a different document or merge multiple source documents into one PDF.
- One source ID should correspond to one identifiable source artifact unless the provider itself publishes a series as separate files.
- If a provider publishes a source only as a webpage and no official downloadable document exists, do not create a PDF by printing the webpage. Record it as a web source instead.
- A source being archived does not mean its claims have been fully reviewed or accepted.


## Alias and identity notes

- **SRC-057 is merged into SRC-006** because both records refer to the same World Bank *Bangladesh Learning Poverty Brief 2024*. Retain SRC-006 as the canonical ID. SRC-074 is a separate World Bank database workbook, specifically the 2021 update (2001–2019 data), and must not be used as the source for SRC-006's 51% estimate. The former “452” substring association with SRC-037 is withdrawn; the 452-person study is Rabby et al. (2023), SRC-067, distinct from SRC-045.
- **SRC-067 is now identified** as Rabby et al. (2023), DOI 10.3389/fpubh.2023.1136557, an online cross-sectional survey of 452 HSC graduates seeking admission. It is distinct from SRC-045, the 210-person tertiary-youth pilot. Neither is a representative survey of all secondary-school students.

## Primary-table verification update — 28 October 2026

The official DPE *National Student Assessment 2022* report was located through the government-hosted PDF mirror and the official DPE publication listing. The executive summary and detailed tables have now been checked. “Grade-level competency” means students in the **Proficient** and **Advanced** performance levels; “Basic” and “Below Basic” are below grade level. Table 25 (report page 34) gives Bangla grade-level attainment: Grade 3, 47% in 2017 and 51% in 2022; Grade 5, 44% in 2017 and 50% in 2022. Do not describe these percentages as the share who can read or do mathematics in every practical sense; they are attainment against the assessment's specified grade-level performance standards.
