# SRC-058 — Singapore MOE: Assessment Reform and Joy of Learning

- **Organization:** Singapore Ministry of Education
- **URL:** https://educationconversations.moe.gov.sg/our-efforts
- **Type:** Official policy summary
- **Reported measures:** Wider PSLE scoring bands; removal of weighted assessments and examinations for Primary 1–2 from 2019; removal of mid-year examinations across primary, secondary and junior college levels by 2025; subject-based banding and interdisciplinary project work.
- **Use:** Official evidence that assessment pressure can be deliberately reduced while retaining important qualifications.
- **Limits:** Policy existence does not prove impact. Singapore differs in governance, resources and social context; examine independent evaluations and unintended effects before borrowing.
- **Status:** Policy source located; outcome evaluation pending.
