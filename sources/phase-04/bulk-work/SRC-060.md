# SRC-060 — OECD PISA 2022 Country Note: Estonia

- **Organization:** OECD
- **Publication:** 5 December 2023
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-i-and-ii-country-notes_ed6fbcc5-en/estonia_dafed886-en.html
- **Type:** International assessment report
- **Population:** Sampled 15-year-olds in Estonia's participating schools.
- **Use:** Compare mathematics, reading, science, proficiency distributions, socioeconomic gaps and trends.
- **Limits:** PISA is partial, not a complete measure of education. It cannot identify a single policy as the cause of country outcomes. Bangladesh did not participate in PISA 2022; do not assign a Bangladesh score.
- **Status:** Country note located; table-level and contextual analysis pending.
