# SRC-059 — Finland National Core Curriculum: Transversal Competences

- **Organization:** Finnish National Agency for Education
- **URL:** https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/national-core-curriculum-primary-and-lower-secondary-basic-education
- **Type:** Official curriculum framework
- **Design features:** Thinking/learning to learn; interaction and expression; self-care and daily-life management; multiliteracy; ICT; working-life competence and entrepreneurship; participation and sustainable futures.
- **Use:** Example of embedding transversal competencies across subjects.
- **Limits:** Curriculum statements show intended design, not causal impact. Finland's institutions and teacher preparation differ from Bangladesh.
- **Status:** Official framework located; implementation and outcome appraisal pending.
