# Step 1 — Research Question, Objectives & Scope

**Author:** Md. Nafiz  
**Phase:** 1 — Research Foundation  
**Status:** Approved (2026-10-09)  
**Official research title:** *Is our education system actually preparing students for the future, or is it mainly preparing students to pass examinations?*

## Purpose

This step established what the research investigates before designing an education system. Its purpose was to prevent scope drift, hidden assumptions, premature solutions, and the mistake of treating the initial question as already answered.

## Approved primary research question

> **What should an education system look like if its purpose is to help people develop the knowledge, capabilities, judgement, character, agency, and foundations needed to learn, live, work, participate in society, and continue developing across their lives?**

The official research title is the motivating question, not an assumption that examination-oriented education is universally ineffective.

## Approved scope and research tasks

The research will examine educational purposes, learners and learning, knowledge, capabilities, curriculum, teaching, assessment, teachers, technology, equity, transitions, governance, implementation, and evaluation.

Bangladesh is the primary contextual case. International evidence will be used for comparison, with attention to context and transferability. The main design target is a coherent pathway from early education through secondary education, connected to post-secondary pathways, work, citizenship, and lifelong learning.

## Evidence strategy

Prioritize primary policy documents, major research reports, peer-reviewed scholarship where relevant, official datasets and measurement frameworks, Bangladesh policy and system evidence, and credible comparative evidence.

Institutional frameworks can help map how organizations conceptualize education, but they are not automatic empirical proof.

## Research safeguards

The research will not assume that examinations are inherently harmful, that technology is inherently beneficial, that another country's system can be copied wholesale, or that a list of future skills is enough to design a curriculum. It will actively seek evidence and counterarguments.

Major claims and recommendations should be traceable to evidence, interpretation, stated values, and acknowledged trade-offs.
