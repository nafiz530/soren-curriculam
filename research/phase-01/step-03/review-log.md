## Important factual caution: policy and commission status

The National Education Policy 2010 is treated as the latest fully adopted national education policy identified in the currently reviewed sources, not as proof that it is wholly implemented or that no later policy action exists. Public reporting in December 2024 described an intention to scrap it and form a commission; reporting on 22 January 2025 quoted the education adviser as saying there was no plan for such a commission. NCTB's official pages document later curriculum revisions, but these do not by themselves prove that a new national policy was adopted or that a commission was formally constituted.

**Required before making a definitive claim:** locate an official gazette, ministry order, membership notice, commission report, or equivalent primary record. The manuscript must distinguish a proposed commission, a formally established commission, an advisory council, and a completed policy process.
