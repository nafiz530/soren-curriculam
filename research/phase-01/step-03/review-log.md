## Known limitations

- This is an initial synthesis, not a systematic review of all educational philosophy.
- The draft does not claim to settle the moral priority of competing purposes.
- Bangladesh policy is treated as stated intent; implementation and outcome evidence are not inferred from it.
- The source set is insufficient for causal claims about which purposes produce which outcomes.
- The evaluation criteria are a research tool, not an approved ranking of values.
