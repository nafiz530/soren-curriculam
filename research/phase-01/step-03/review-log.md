## Important factual caution: policy and commission status

The National Education Policy 2010 is treated as the latest fully adopted national education policy identified in the currently reviewed sources, while its present status remains distinct from the status of curricula and reform bodies. Public reporting described a proposed broad commission, but later reporting said no sector-wide education commission was constituted during the interim government's first year. At the same time, the Primary and Non-Formal Education Reform Advisory Committee submitted recommendations in February 2025, and a separate secondary/higher-secondary review committee report was published in February 2026. Current government representatives have also described plans for a new primary policy and a 2028 curriculum redesign.

**Required before making a definitive claim:** distinguish a broad education-sector commission, specialized advisory/review committees, a proposed policy, an adopted policy, and an implemented curriculum. The existence of a committee report does not establish implementation, and a public plan does not establish formal adoption.
