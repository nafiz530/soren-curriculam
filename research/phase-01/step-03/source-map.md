## Evidence gaps

1. Use the philosophical overview to identify original scholarship on flourishing, autonomy, democratic education, knowledge, cultural transmission, and economic aims.
2. Review Bangladesh policy aims against the primary document before asserting specific provisions.
3. The draft maps normative purposes more strongly than it evaluates outcomes. Empirical claims about consequences belong in later steps with suitable evidence.
4. Institutional frameworks are disproportionately represented. Add critical scholarship on competency frameworks, human-capital reductionism, universalized models, and vague holistic aims.
5. Aims and indicators must remain distinct: the easiest outcome to measure is not automatically the most important purpose.

## Traceability rule

Any proposed design principle in a later phase should identify the relevant purpose, the evidence or normative argument supporting it, its interpretation, its trade-offs, and plausible failure modes. This step does not authorize a final hierarchy of educational aims.
