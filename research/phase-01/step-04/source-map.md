## Methodological limitations

- PRISMA was designed for transparent reporting of systematic reviews, particularly reviews of studies evaluating health interventions; it is not a complete method for normative philosophy or every type of education research.
- WWC standards are education-specific but developed for a defined review remit. They should inform, not mechanically determine, appraisal of historical, qualitative, philosophical, or Bangladesh policy sources.
- The Magenta Book is UK government guidance. Its evaluation concepts may be useful elsewhere, but institutional context and transferability must be assessed.
- AERA's Code of Ethics is professional guidance, not a replacement for Bangladesh law or local institutional/guardian permissions.
- The protocol is written for a student-led research project with limited time and access. Searches may not cover all databases, languages, unpublished work, or paywalled literature.
- The protocol itself must be tested against actual use. If it creates excessive paperwork without improving traceability, simplify it transparently rather than abandoning the underlying safeguards.

## Traceability rule

A source's inclusion in this map records its intended methodological use, not blanket endorsement. Each later claim must still be checked against the source and the claim's scope.
