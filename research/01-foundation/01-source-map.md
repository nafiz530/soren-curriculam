# Step 1 — Source Map

This map links the Step 1 questions to the source base. It is intentionally incomplete and will grow during research.

| Question area | Initial sources | What they can contribute | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose of education | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-004, SRC-005 | Major institutional framings of educational purpose and outcomes | These are frameworks/strategies, not a single empirical answer |
| Future orientation | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-005 | Alternative futures, agency, competencies, learning and system transformation | Future-oriented claims must be distinguished from forecasts |
| Bangladesh policy purpose | SRC-007 | Bangladesh's formal policy aims and stated educational priorities | Policy intent is not evidence of implementation or outcomes |
| Bangladesh learning conditions | SRC-006 | Country-level evidence on foundational learning and system conditions | Measures specific outcomes and time periods; cannot represent all educational outcomes |
| System conditions | SRC-008 | Indicators and policy drivers associated with foundational learning | Association does not automatically establish causation |
| Counterarguments | To be expanded | Critical scholarship and evidence that challenges the initial framing | Must be actively searched, not added only when found accidentally |

## Source handling rule

Every major claim in `01-main.md` should eventually be traceable to one or more source IDs or explicitly marked as:

- working definition;
- project decision;
- hypothesis;
- interpretation;
- proposal.

No claim should be given the appearance of evidence merely because it appears in the main narrative.
