# DEC-004-001 — Claim-Relative Evidence Threshold

**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** Approved working standard  
**Related step:** research/phase-01/step-04/  
**Decision owner:** Md. Nafiz

## Decision
Use **established evidence** only when either (a) at least two independent lines converge, using distinct datasets, methods, or research groups as appropriate; or (b) one high-quality study closely matches the claim's construct, population, setting, and design. In both cases, document an active search for credible counterevidence. Major recommendations require the complete traceability chain.

## Rationale
A universal numerical evidence score would obscure differences between conceptual, descriptive, causal, historical, and normative claims. The threshold must be relative to claim scope and causal requirements.

## Application
- Multiple publications repeating one dataset or original statement do not count as independent.
- The population and setting must share the causal features on which the claim depends.
- If the threshold is unmet, use a narrower label such as finding, expert judgment, limited/mixed evidence, hypothesis, or speculation.
- State scope and limitations; do not imply universal truth.
- Record why the threshold is satisfied in the evidence table.

## Revisit condition
Revisit if real application shows the rule misclassifies claims or is impractical. Log examples and rationale for any change.
