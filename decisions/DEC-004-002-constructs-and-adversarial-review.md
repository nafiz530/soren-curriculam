# DEC-004-002 — Operational Constructs and Adversarial Review

**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** Approved working standard  
**Related step:** research/phase-01/step-04/  
**Decision owner:** Md. Nafiz

## Decision A — Operational constructs
Before title-level synthesis, define “preparing students for the future” and “preparing students to pass examinations” as multidimensional constructs with observable indicators or qualitative evidence. For every source, record its own definition, measure, population, setting, time horizon, and degree of match to the project's working definition. Similar wording alone does not make studies commensurable.

## Decision B — Adversarial review
Every high-impact conclusion or design-driving claim needs a documented devil's-advocate pass in the relevant review log. Record the strongest credible opposing case, evidence for it, assumptions of the preferred interpretation, missing evidence, and what would change the conclusion. AI review cannot be the only substantive review.

## Decision C — Normative disagreement
State value premises, record strongest counterarguments, explain weights assigned to competing considerations, and apply Step 3's evaluation criteria. Preserve reasonable unresolved disagreement rather than presenting a value weighting as an empirical result.

## Revisit condition
Revisit after the first substantive synthesis if construct matching or adversarial review proves unclear. Preserve the audit trail for changes.
